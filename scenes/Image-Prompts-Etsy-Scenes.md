# Image prompts for Etsy mockup scenes

I composite the finished art into these. They must come out with EMPTY frames, paper, tags and screens.

## Rules for every image
- Format: PNG preferred (JPG at maximum quality is fine). No watermarks, no text anywhere in the scene.
- Size: as listed per scene. Anything over 2400 px on the long edge is fine. Do not crop or upscale afterwards.
- Camera: 50mm lens look, camera at the centre height of the frame, straight on (no wide-angle distortion, no tilt) unless the scene says otherwise.
- Every frame, paper, tag, bauble or screen must be completely visible, uncropped, with all four edges and corners in shot.
- Frame interiors: plain flat white, no reflections of objects, no artwork, no text. A soft diffuse window light across the glass is fine.
- Save to Etsy-Shop/_photos using the file name shown (S01.png and so on), or attach them here.
- Style for all: real photograph, natural light, shallow depth of field, calm Australian home, muted neutral palette.

Add this to the end of every prompt: "Photorealistic interior photograph, natural window light, high detail, no text, no watermark, no people, no artwork inside frames."
